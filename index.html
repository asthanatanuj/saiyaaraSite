About the team

A team built on rhythm, culture, and family.

Saiyaara is San Jose State's competitive Bollywood fusion dance team, blending Bollywood, classical Indian, and hip-hop styles into one high-energy performance style. We compete on the national collegiate Bollywood fusion circuit and perform across the Bay Area throughout the year.
