ABOUT SAIYAARA

Built for the Dance Community.

Saiyaara is San Jose State University's inaugural Bollywood-fusion collegiate dance competition.

Based in Silicon Valley, the competition brings collegiate dance teams together to compete, connect, and inspire through South Asian artistry and cultural identity.

The inaugural competition is scheduled for March 6, 2027 at Mexican Heritage Plaza in San Jose, California.
