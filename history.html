SAIYAARA COMMUNITY

Growing Together

2026

Building the next Saiyaara

Saiyaara continues to grow as SJSU's collegiate Bollywood Fusion competition, bringing together student teams, performers, organizers, sponsors, and the wider dance community. Details for the 2026 competition, participating teams, and results will be announced as they are finalized.

2025

Expanding the vision

Saiyaara continued developing its competition experience and community at San José State University, with a focus on creating a stronger platform for collegiate South Asian dance and bringing universities together through performance and competition.

2024

Saiyaara begins at San José State University

Saiyaara was established at San José State University with the goal of creating a collegiate Bollywood Fusion competition that celebrates South Asian culture, gives student performers a stage to showcase their talent, and connects university dance teams through friendly competition.
