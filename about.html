The People Behind Saiyaara

Saiyaara is a student-run collegiate Bollywood Fusion dance competition at San José State University.

Created to celebrate South Asian culture and collegiate performance, Saiyaara brings university dance teams together for a competition centered around creativity, community, and friendly rivalry.

Behind the stage, SJSU students work across production, hospitality, outreach, sponsorships, marketing, and competition logistics to create an experience that performers and audiences can remember.

Community Engagement

Workshops & Community Events

Saiyaara engages with the broader community through workshops, outreach programs, and cultural events that celebrate South Asian dance and foster connections across campus and beyond.
