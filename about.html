WHY SAIYAARA EXISTS

A new platform for South Asian performance.

Saiyaara is San Jose State University's inaugural Bollywood-fusion collegiate dance competition, created as a high-energy celebration of South Asian artistry, cultural identity, and community impact.

Based in Silicon Valley, the competition is designed to bring collegiate dance teams from across the country together to compete, connect, and inspire.

Saiyaara means "shooting star" in Hindi. The name reflects a rising platform intended to empower the next generation of South Asian performers and leaders.

Community Engagement

Workshops & Community Events

Our community includes college students, young professionals, and families with strong ties to the South Asian community. Our reach spans the Bay Area and extends nationally through competing universities and the collegiate dance network.
