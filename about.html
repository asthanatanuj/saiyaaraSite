WHY SAIYAARA EXISTS

A stage with a purpose beyond placements.

Saiyaara is a student-run collegiate Bollywood Fusion dance competition at San José State University. We bring university teams together to tell stories through movement, music, production, and the many South Asian dance traditions that shape the fusion circuit.

Competition creates the spark, but community gives the event meaning. We want visiting dancers to feel welcomed, audiences to feel connected to the performances, and student organizers to leave with the experience of building something ambitious together.

Every detail is led by students—from technical production and hospitality to finance, outreach, sponsorships, registration, and marketing. That collaboration is what turns a single performance night into a lasting campus tradition.

Community Engagement

Workshops & Community Events

Saiyaara's community is built throughout the year. Workshops make dance more approachable, campus events create space to share culture, and collaborations with student organizations connect people who may never otherwise meet. These moments help new dancers learn, experienced performers exchange ideas, and the wider SJSU community take part in the story.
