THE COMPETITION

A Stage for Collegiate Bollywood Fusion.

Saiyaara brings university Bollywood Fusion teams together at San José State University for a celebration of performance, culture, and friendly competition. Teams take the stage representing their universities, showcasing months of creativity, choreography, production, and preparation in front of the collegiate dance community. Our goal is to create a competition experience that supports performers while giving audiences the energy and excitement that make collegiate Bollywood Fusion unique.

SAIYAARA COMMUNITY

Built by Community

More Than a Competition.

Saiyaara is built around the community behind the performances — the dancers, organizers, universities, supporters, and audiences who make collegiate South Asian dance possible.
