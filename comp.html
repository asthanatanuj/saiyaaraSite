THE COMPETITION

A Stage for Collegiate Bollywood Fusion.

Saiyaara brings university teams to San José State University to share original stories, ambitious choreography, and the energy of collegiate Bollywood Fusion.

SAIYAARA COMMUNITY

Built by Community

UNDERSTANDING THE CIRCUIT

More Than a Competition.

Teams spend months building a themed set that blends dance styles, music, storytelling, costumes, and production. On stage, every formation and transition reflects that shared work.

01

Bollywood Fusion

A performance format that brings Bollywood together with styles such as hip-hop, classical Indian dance, contemporary, folk, and more. Each team uses that mix to create a distinct narrative and visual identity.

02

The DDN Community

Desi Dance Network is a nonprofit community supporting South Asian performance artists and the collegiate dance circuit through competitions, education, connection, and artistic development.

03

What We Represent

Saiyaara aims to represent thoughtful hospitality, creative freedom, cultural pride, and a competition experience where every visiting team feels respected from arrival through the final bow.

THE SAIYAARA EXPERIENCE

Built for performers and audiences.

For dancers, that means clear communication, organized logistics, dependable technical support, and a team that understands how much preparation it took to reach the stage.

For audiences, it means an immersive night of music, movement, culture, and stories that are easy to follow even if it is your first Bollywood Fusion competition.
